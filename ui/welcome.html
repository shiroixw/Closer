Site access

Closer needs to see every page to decide which images are worth opening — that's the whole product. It reads nothing else: no page text, no form values, no history, no cookies. No network requests, no analytics. You can revoke this here or from chrome://extensions at any time.

Paused sites —

    Nothing paused. When a site has its own viewer or gets in the way, hit Pause on … inside the info panel.
